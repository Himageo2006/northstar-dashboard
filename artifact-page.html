Where these numbers come from

Google Play: Play Console app list, last 28 days to 10 Nov (device acquisitions and monthly active devices). Play shows no store sales because none of the apps sold in-app items there.

App Store: App Store Connect Analytics, first-time downloads and proceeds per day. Mud Engineer Pro went live on iOS on 10 Nov, so it has no downloads yet.

In-app ads: LevelPlay performance report, revenue, impressions and ad users per app and day, 12 Sep to 10 Nov. Only Benny's Farm and Kingdom Rise show ads.

Apple Ads: campaign report, last 7 days to 11 Nov. YouTube: YouTube Studio channel analytics, 14 Oct to 10 Nov.
